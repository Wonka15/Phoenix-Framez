Your camera stays under your control

Camera access starts only after you tap “Turn camera on” and approve your browser's permission prompt. PhoenixFramez uses the camera locally in this browser, with audio disabled. The camera station does not upload or stream your camera feed to PhoenixFramez servers. Captured frames are saved in this browser's local project storage; exporting or sharing a project is your choice. Turn the camera off at any time to stop the camera tracks.

Blender

Bidirectional live sync: scrub the PhoenixFramez timeline and Blender follows; scrub Blender and PhoenixFramez follows. Exchange supported scene, frame, range and marker changes through the optional local bridge.

First targets: Blender 5.3 stable and 5.2 LTS · Local bridge required
